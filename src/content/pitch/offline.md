# The critic, offline

---

## A critic you can quote

Security claims are usually binary: "above a threshold, so we are fine." That is a threshold
claim — a spectrum claim with the tail cut off and labelled safe. A critic refuses that.

---

## The spectrum, not the flag

Every protection is a continuous knob: rotation, rate limit, gate, surface grant, decoys. Turning
a knob down is a *drop* in security, never a binary state change. A test is valid only when it
matches its declared curve.

---

## The model

A citation is an immutable event anchored at 42 = 0. Local-time's derivation phase separates
verified isotope events from background noise. The critic synthesizes a mixed stream and
demodulates it.

---

## Four failure modes

Jitter-vs-tampering, tri-state collapse, side-channel bleed, sybil flooding. Each is pass, drift,
or violation — measured, not asserted.

---

## Four verification criteria

Separability, threshold, projection fidelity, and the forced-inclusion canary. The canary is the
cheapest blue move: inject a log, force validation to include it.

---

## Reproducible, or it is nothing

Same seed, same verdicts. The freshness gate refuses a build whose page cannot state its snapshot.
A critic that disagrees with itself is a critic nobody can quote.
